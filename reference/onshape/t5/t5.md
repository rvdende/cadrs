## Behaviour notes (from the pages)
- **Use (U):** "Project (or convert) an edge, edges, and silhouette edges of a part or sketch onto
  the active sketch plane." Hovering shows a preview of the projection. Silhouettes: hovering a
  face shows them; clicking a face takes all, clicking one takes it. "All used edges update when the
  underlying geometry changes." Silhouette ends are not constrained.
- **Sketch text:** "Click and drag to establish the position and size of the text box. (The lower
  left corner and the height define the text position and size of the first line of text.) Note
  that a horizontal constraint is applied to the lower edge of the box by default." Up to 250
  characters; font, Bold, Italic; flips about the frame's horizontal and vertical centres; Edit
  text from the context menu. "The box represents the line length and height (text baseline to
  text ascender)." To rotate: remove the Horizontal, fix a corner, drag another. Only one of
  width/height can be dimensioned.
- **Pierce (Shift+G):** "Constrain a sketch entity (point or curve) to be coincident with the
  intersection point of its sketch plane and an arbitrary curve that is not in its sketch plane."
  Pick a sketch point and an edge through the plane; the tool stays on for further pairs.
- **Imprinting:** with "Disable imprinting" unchecked, the face's edges take part in the sketch's
  regions; checking it keeps the sketch from being split by them.

## cadrs choices
- Projected curves have their own points (edges that meet do not share one), are fixed, black,
  and carry a light-blue (#92d4ee) link glyph; deleting it leaves free geometry.
- Imprinted edges are region boundaries only (not curves, not pickable). A region bounded only by
  a face's own edges is not a sketch region; overlaps with sketch geometry are.
- Text fonts: the Inter faces cadrs ships (Inter, Medium, SemiBold, Black); Bold uses a heavier
  Inter face, Italic is a 12° slant. The box height is the capitals' height.
