## Downloads

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux x86_64 (glibc 2.35+: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or newer) | `cadrs-v0.2.8-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` |
| Windows x86_64 (Windows 10 or 11) | `cadrs-v0.2.8-x86_64-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel | `cadrs-v0.2.8-universal-macos.dmg` |
| A browser with WebGPU or WebGL2 (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) | <https://cadrs.net/app/>, or `cadrs-v0.2.8-web.tar.gz` to serve yourself |

The Linux and Windows archives hold the executable, its `libraries/` folder (the built-in symbols, footprints and 3D models), the README and the licenses. Nothing needs installing: unpack it and run `cadrs` (Linux) or `cadrs.exe` (Windows), keeping `libraries/` beside it. `SHA256SUMS.txt` has the files' checksums.

On macOS, open the `.dmg` and drag cadrs to Applications. The app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS won't open it: right-click cadrs in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (on macOS 15 and later, open it once, then choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security). Updates on macOS are downloaded from the release page and dragged over the old app.

On Linux, the app needs a Vulkan-capable GPU driver, and ALSA and udev (present on desktop distributions). The Windows build isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Documents are saved in `~/.local/share/cadrs/` on Linux, `%APPDATA%\cadrs\` on Windows and `~/Library/Application Support/cadrs/` on macOS.

## What's new since v0.2.7

- **Gears** (#228). A Gear feature, and a Gear pair made as one feature from its ratio: a hypoid pair, or a worm drive whose wheel is hobbed by its worm (1 to 4 starts). Bevel gears, worm wheels, face gears and spiroids are cut by their mating gear as exact faces; on [brep-bench](https://brepbench.org) all five gear cases now build as exact B-rep. Fast previews follow the cutter while anything is dragged, and a pair says where its teeth interfere.
- **The site in more browsers** (#227, #234). <https://cadrs.net/> runs in Safari again (on a Mac and on iPad), and in browsers without WebGPU (Chrome on Linux, Firefox, older Safari), drawn with WebGL2.
- **Shell keeps its walls** (#240). A shell whose removed face is overhung by a kept one no longer cuts that wall short, inward or outward; the results match Onshape's. Rounded outer corners are now an option, off by default.
- **Sketches** (#238, #244). Double-click a sketch to look at it square on, fitted beside its dialog; F frames just the selected part of a sketch. Perpendicular corners show a right-angle mark, coincident points are solid squares (right-click to delete the constraint), constraint glyphs sit outside the shape, and tangents are marked where the curves touch. Drags keep radii.
- **Thumbnails** (#236). A document's thumbnail follows it while it is open, is exported with it, and shows Assemblies too.
- **Kernel** (#239). STEP faces that are fully transparent in FreeCAD's sense read as opaque, Measure shows the radius of fillets read from STEP, and lofts and extrusions handle more near-degenerate inputs.

cadrs is an independent project.
