Public documents (publish, find, star and fork, peer to peer), the interface in eleven languages, Send to slicer, surfacing curves, walk mode and faster rebuilds.

## Downloads

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux x86_64 (glibc 2.35+: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or newer) | `cadrs-v0.2.5-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` |
| Windows x86_64 (Windows 10 or 11) | `cadrs-v0.2.5-x86_64-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel | `cadrs-v0.2.5-universal-macos.dmg` |
| A browser with WebGPU, such as Chrome or Edge | <https://cadrs.net/app/>, or `cadrs-v0.2.5-web.tar.gz` to serve yourself |

The Linux and Windows archives hold the executable, its `libraries/` folder (the built-in symbols, footprints and 3D models), the README and the licenses. Nothing needs installing: unpack it and run `cadrs` (Linux) or `cadrs.exe` (Windows), keeping `libraries/` beside it. `SHA256SUMS.txt` has the files' checksums.

On macOS, open the `.dmg` and drag cadrs to Applications. The app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS won't open it: right-click cadrs in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (on macOS 15 and later, open it once, then choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security). Updates on macOS are downloaded from the release page and dragged over the old app.

On Linux, the app needs a Vulkan-capable GPU driver, and ALSA and udev (present on desktop distributions). The Windows build isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Documents are saved in `~/.local/share/cadrs/` on Linux, `%APPDATA%\cadrs\` on Windows and `~/Library/Application Support/cadrs/` on macOS.

## What's new since v0.2.4

- **Public documents** (#196). Share › **Public** publishes a document as it is, for anyone using cadrs to find, view and fork; nobody else can edit it, and later edits stay private until you publish again.
  - The documents page has a **Public documents** panel: everyone's public documents by stars or newest, with a search. A row opens the document read-only; ★ stars it; **Fork** saves a copy of your own.
  - It's peer to peer: listings and stars are signed and passed between cadrs users, and the documents themselves come from their owners or from a seed peer that keeps them while owners are offline (`cadrs --public-seed`). cadrs finds other users through `peers.cadrs.net`, which keeps only who is online.
  - Only while you're online (the network button), and Preferences › Collaboration can leave public documents out.
- **The interface in eleven languages** (#178): German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Afrikaans, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified), in Preferences › Language. The translations are drafts, to be reviewed by native speakers.
- **Send to slicer** (#176): a part's right-click menu sends its mesh straight into PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio, UltiMaker Cura, SuperSlicer or Creality Print installed on the machine (or a program of your own, `CADRS_SLICER`).
- **Surfacing** (#197): lofts through curves with guides, projected curves, splitting faces along a curve, face blends, sweeps of curve profiles, and surfaces that join the surfaces they meet. They come in from imported documents; the dialogs for projected curves and face blends come later.
- **The view** (#97):
  - **Zoom to selection** (Alt+F, Numpad .) makes the selection the pivot the view orbits about.
  - **Keep camera up** (the view menu) turns about the vertical without rolling.
  - **Walk mode** moves through a building from inside, with W A S D, the mouse to look and the wheel for the lens.
- **Faster rebuilds and editing** (#192): full rebuilds about 2.5 to 3 times faster on large documents, and an edit no longer freezes the app while sketches on faces regenerate.
- **Values and dimensions**:
  - The mouse wheel steps a value being edited, and the preview follows at once (#162).
  - Sketch angles turn like a hinge, distances can go negative, and a variable that drives a dimension out of range says so (#96).
  - The feature list shows each variable's value, and feature dialogs list variables on `#`.
- **Assemblies**: dragging a long chain of mated parts no longer breaks the mates (#191), and Full rebuild rebuilds an assembly's source Part Studios.
- **Around the app** (#175):
  - The Parts list has folders.
  - Tab folders open as lists from the tab bar, and a tab shows a card when hovered.
  - Comments, History and Share are in the top bar, and the top bar folds on a narrow window.
  - New documents start with a Part Studio only.
- **MCP**: `get_selection` tells an assistant what is selected in the 3D view (#177).
